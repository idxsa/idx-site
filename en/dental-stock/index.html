Features

Everything a dental clinic needs, in one app

Designed with a real dental clinic team, and works the way assistants, dentists and clinic managers already work. Arabic-first, right-to-left interface.

Dispense and receive by camera

Snap the item or its barcode — the app recognizes it and deducts the quantity. A supplier's invoice is read from a single photo.

Know when each supply runs out

It calculates usage from dispensing history and shows the days left before each item runs out, so no chair is ever stopped.

Lab cases

Track every case — sent, received or returned for a redo — with due-date reminders and a ready-made message to the lab.

Per-patient cost

Log the materials used in each visit and see the real cost of treatment to the clinic.

One team, one stock

The clinic manager invites staff, and every change syncs instantly across devices via iCloud.

Alerts and widgets

Alerts when any item runs low or nears expiry, plus a widget that shows shortages on your home screen.

Screens

A calm, clear interface — light or dark

Privacy

The clinic's data stays with the clinic

In the clinic's iCloud

Items, transactions and lab cases are stored in the clinic's account and never pass through our servers.

On-device recognition

Barcodes and text are read on the device itself. AI features are optional and use the clinic's own key.

No ads, no tracking

No ads and no usage-data collection. Privacy policy
