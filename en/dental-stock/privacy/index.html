Privacy policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

This policy explains how the Dental Stock app, developed and published by Al Tahawul Al Raqmi Al Dhaki Company (iDX), Unified National Number 7051364813, Riyadh, handles the data of clinics and their users.

Where clinic data is stored

Items, transactions, lab cases and patient records entered by the clinic are stored in the clinic's own iCloud account through Apple's CloudKit, under Apple's privacy policy. This data never passes through our servers, and we cannot access it.

Sharing with the clinic team

When the clinic manager invites a staff member, they can view and edit the clinic's stock through secure iCloud sharing. The manager can revoke access at any time.

Camera and on-device recognition

The app uses the camera to read barcodes, item photos and invoices; recognition happens on the device itself. We do not store or receive these images.

AI features (optional)

If a clinic enables AI features with its own key, the image or text to be analyzed is sent directly from the device to the provider the clinic chose, under that provider's terms and policy. This data does not pass through us, and the feature can be turned off at any time.

Subscriptions and payment

Subscriptions are handled by the App Store. We do not receive payment or card details; Apple manages billing, renewal and cancellation.

Ads and tracking

The app contains no ads, uses no tracking tools, and does not collect usage or analytics data.

Deleting data

Any item or record can be deleted inside the app. To delete all app data, remove it from iCloud settings on your device (Manage Storage, then Dental Stock), or delete the app together with its data. Because we keep no copy, there is no data held by us to delete.

Changes
