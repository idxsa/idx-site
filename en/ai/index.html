Frequently asked questions

Will the agent make things up?

We restrict the agent to business information we approve with you, and set it to say it doesn't know and hand over to an employee rather than guess. We review its conversations during the trial.

Where does the agent work?

On WhatsApp first, and it can be added to your website or internal systems to support your team.

Does it integrate with our existing systems?

Yes, wherever the system offers a way to connect. We link it to your calendar, spreadsheets or order system so it can carry out tasks itself.

What about customer data privacy?

We process data in line with Saudi Arabia's Personal Data Protection Law, and the agent only sees what it needs for its task.

Does it understand Saudi dialect?

Yes. It understands Saudi dialect, Modern Standard Arabic and English, and replies in the style we agree on for your business.

How is it different from ChatGPT?

ChatGPT is a general assistant that answers anything. Your agent is restricted to your business's information and policies, connected to your systems, and works on your business's WhatsApp number under its name.

How much does it cost?

It depends on how much information it covers, the tasks it handles and the systems we connect it to. After a free consultation you get a clear quote. Meta's message fees are separate and paid directly to Meta.
