Google Business Profile guide: get your business on Google Maps and local search

When customers search for "a company near me" or for a service in their city, Google first shows businesses that have a Google Business Profile. Creating one is free, and it's one of the fastest ways to show up in local search.

iDX team · 14 November 2026

What is a Google Business Profile?

A free profile that presents your business in Google Search and Maps: name, category, hours, phone number, website, photos and reviews. The profile only appears to the public after it's verified.

Before you start: do customers visit you?

  • A place customers visit: such as a clinic, restaurant or shop. Your address is shown, and the place needs signage with your business name and staff during working hours.
  • A service-area business: if you serve customers at their locations or remotely and nobody visits you. Your address is hidden, and you set the areas you serve.

Choosing the wrong type is one of the most common reasons for failed verification or a suspended profile.

Steps to create your profile

  1. Go to business.google.com/add with the Google account you want to manage the business from.
  2. Enter your business name as it is in real life, without adding words like "best" or the city name.
  3. Choose the primary category closest to your business; you can add more categories later.
  4. Say whether customers visit you, or set your service area. Google allows up to 20 areas and recommends keeping the service area within two hours' drive of your base.
  5. Add your phone number and website link.
  6. Complete verification with the method Google offers, which is often a live video.

Video verification

Google often asks for a live video, not a pre-recorded one, showing three things: where your business is, proof the business exists such as signage or your commercial registration, and proof that you own or manage it. Verification review can take up to five business days.

After verification: complete your profile

  • Description: say clearly what you offer, to whom, and where.
  • Services: add each service with a short description.
  • Hours: enter your real hours and update them for holidays.
  • Photos: your logo, a cover photo, and photos of your work and premises.
  • Reviews: ask satisfied customers for a review, and reply politely to every review, including negative ones.

Common mistakes to avoid

  • Adding search keywords to the business name.
  • Creating more than one profile for the same business in the same city.
  • Using a PO box or virtual office address.
  • Leaving the profile without updates or review replies.
