AI WhatsApp bots: can you connect ChatGPT to your business WhatsApp?

Many people search for a "ChatGPT WhatsApp bot" to answer their customers with AI. It's possible, but the rules changed in 2026. Here's what you need to know before you start.

iDX team · 14 November 2026

What changed in 2026?

Since 15 January 2026, the terms of Meta's WhatsApp Business Platform prohibit general-purpose assistants that act as a personal assistant answering any question, such as ChatGPT and Perplexity, and they're no longer available through WhatsApp. Bots that serve the business, such as customer service, bookings, FAQs and order tracking, are still allowed, including bots built with AI.

A general assistant or your business's agent?

  • General assistant: answers anything on any topic. This is what Meta prohibits on the business platform.
  • Your business's agent: uses AI to understand your customers' messages, but answers only from your business information and carries out your business's tasks. This is allowed, and it's what you actually need.

How does an AI agent work on WhatsApp?

  1. Your business number is connected to the official WhatsApp Business Platform.
  2. The agent is connected to a language model that understands messages as customers write them, in Arabic, Saudi dialect or English.
  3. It's fed only your business information: services, prices, policies and FAQs.
  4. It gets clear limits: what it answers, what it hands to a person, and which tasks it may perform.
  5. It's connected to your systems so it can book or create orders itself.

The risks and how to avoid them

  • Made-up information: language models can guess a wrong answer. The fix is to restrict the agent to your business information and set it to say it doesn't know and hand over, rather than guess.
  • Off-topic questions: if a customer asks about something unrelated to your business, it apologizes and steers back to what concerns you.
  • Data privacy: customer data should be processed under Saudi Arabia's Personal Data Protection Law, and the agent should only see what it needs.
  • Review: review the agent's conversations in the first weeks and adjust what needs adjusting.

When do you need an AI agent instead of a regular bot?

A regular bot with flows and buttons is enough for bookings and sorting. An agent suits you better when questions are many and varied, and customers phrase them in ways that are hard to fit into menus. You can also combine both in one bot.

The bottom line

You can't run ChatGPT as-is on your business WhatsApp, but you can build your own business AI agent on the official platform. Explore the iDX AI agent, or compare it with the WhatsApp bot.

Frequently asked questions

Can I connect ChatGPT to my business WhatsApp?

A general assistant like ChatGPT can't run on the WhatsApp Business Platform since 15 January 2026. But you can build an AI agent that serves your business and answers from its information.

Does an AI agent understand Saudi dialect?

Yes. Modern language models understand Saudi dialect, Modern Standard Arabic and English, and the reply style is set for your business.
