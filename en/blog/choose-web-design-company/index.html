How to choose a web design company in Riyadh: 8 questions before you sign

A website is an investment that stays with you for years, and choosing the wrong company means a slow site that doesn't show up on Google or bring customers. Here are eight questions we recommend asking any company before you sign, including us.

iDX team · 14 November 2026

1. Can I see real websites you've built?

Ask for links to live websites, not just images. Open them on your phone and check their speed and how easy it is to get in touch. If you can, ask their owners about their experience.

2. Is the site built on a ready-made template or coded for my business?

Templates aren't always wrong, but they're often heavy and look like thousands of other sites. Ask how your site will be built, and whether it'll be fast on mobile.

3. How fast is the site on mobile?

Most website visitors in Saudi Arabia come from their phones, and Google takes speed into account when ranking results. Ask them to test one of their previous sites with Google's PageSpeed Insights in front of you.

4. What exactly do you do for SEO?

"SEO-friendly" isn't enough. Ask for details: do they write a title and description for every page? Do they add a sitemap and structured data? Do they register the site in Google Search Console? Do they connect it to your Google Business Profile?

5. Is the site done properly in Arabic and English?

An Arabic site needs correct right-to-left text direction, clear Arabic fonts, and both versions linked so Google knows which one to show each visitor.

6. Are the domain, hosting and accounts in my name?

Make sure the domain is registered in your business's name, and that you can access the hosting and analytics accounts. Otherwise you'll find yourself tied to the company even if you want to move.

7. How does a visitor become a customer?

A beautiful site that doesn't bring contact won't help you. Ask: how will visitors contact me? Is there a WhatsApp button with a ready message? Will I know how many visitors got in touch?

8. What's the total cost, not just the design price?

Ask about everything: the domain, yearly hosting, changes after delivery, support and content. And ask for a written quote with the scope of work and a timeline for each phase.
