Is there a free WhatsApp bot? The free options and their limits

"Free WhatsApp bot" is one of the most common searches from business owners. The short answer: there are free tools that are enough at the start, but a real bot that books, sorts and connects to your systems has a cost. Here's what you get for free, and where free stops.

iDX team · 14 November 2026

1. Free tools in the WhatsApp Business app

The WhatsApp Business app is free and includes simple automatic replies:

  • Greeting message: sent when a customer messages you for the first time.
  • Away message: sent outside working hours.
  • Quick replies: saved replies staff can send with a shortcut.
  • Catalog and labels: to show products and organize chats.

These tools are useful, but they aren't a bot: they don't ask customers what they need, don't book, and don't route them to a department.

2. Free trials on bot platforms

Many bot platforms and providers offer a free trial. It's the best way to see whether a bot suits your business, but it ends in a paid subscription, so ask about the price after the trial before you start.

3. Unofficial auto-reply tools

There are free programs and add-ons that make the regular WhatsApp app reply automatically. They may look like an easy fix, but they break WhatsApp's terms, can get your number banned, and you lose your customer conversations with it. We don't recommend them for any business.

4. Is the WhatsApp API free?

Partly. Under Meta's pricing in effect since October 2026, each number gets 1,000 free reply messages a month inside the customer service window, meaning within 24 hours of the customer's last message. After that each message is charged, and template messages such as marketing and notifications are charged too. On top of that is the cost of the bot itself, since the platform doesn't come with a ready-made bot.

When is free enough?

  • You get few messages, and one person replies to them.
  • Most questions repeat and saved replies cover them.
  • You don't need bookings or a connection to another system.

When do you need a real bot?

  • You get many messages and fall behind on replies.
  • You lose customers outside working hours.
  • Several staff or departments work on the same number.
  • You want automatic bookings, sorting or reminders.

The bottom line

Start with the free WhatsApp Business tools and avoid unofficial programs. When you need more than saved replies, try a WhatsApp bot on the official platform before committing. Also read 7 criteria for choosing the best WhatsApp bot.
