Frequently asked questions

Do you run campaigns on my budget or pay for them?

The ad budget is paid directly from your own ad account, and we charge the agreed management fee. That way the accounts and results stay yours.

What's the minimum budget to start?

It depends on the platform, city and industry. Once we understand your goal we suggest a test budget, then scale it based on results.

Do you shoot on location?

Yes. We arrange shoots of your products, premises or team in Riyadh, and outside Riyadh by arrangement.

How do I know a campaign worked?

We agree on the goal up front, such as conversations or bookings, and send you regular reports with the numbers.

Which platform suits my business best?

It depends on your audience and goal. We suggest the best platform once we understand your business, and may start with two and focus on whichever performs better.

When will I see campaign results?

Messages and visits start in the first days after launch, and the picture becomes clear after the testing period, once we know which ads and audiences actually bring customers.

Do you write content in Arabic and Saudi dialect?

Yes. We write ads and posts in Arabic, Saudi dialect or English, depending on your audience.
