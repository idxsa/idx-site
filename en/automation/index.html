Frequently asked questions

Which tasks can be automated?

Any task repeated with the same steps: moving data between systems, sending alerts and reminders, preparing reports and updating spreadsheets.

Does automation work with my current tools?

Yes. We work with Google Workspace, WhatsApp, ad platforms and any system that offers an API.

What if an automation stops working?

We add alerts that tell us when something fails, and we monitor and fix it.

Do I need a developer on my team?

No. We build and monitor the automation; your team just uses the result.
