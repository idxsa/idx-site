Frequently asked questions

Do I need a new number?

No. Your current business number can be connected to Meta's official WhatsApp Business Platform. Before we start, we explain what the connection requires and how it affects how you use the number today. We can also set up a new number if you prefer.

Can the bot reply in Saudi dialect?

Yes. We write the replies in your business's voice, in Saudi dialect, Modern Standard Arabic or English, and refine them with you.

What if a customer asks something the bot doesn't know?

It hands the chat to an employee with a summary, so the customer doesn't have to repeat themselves.

What's the difference between the bot and the AI agent?

The bot follows set paths we design with you, which suits booking and qualifying. The AI agent understands open questions and answers from your business information. You can combine both.

How much does it cost?

It depends on the number of departments and flows. We start with a 14-day free trial, then a fixed monthly subscription agreed before we begin. Meta's message fees are separate from the subscription and paid directly to Meta.

Are automatic replies allowed on WhatsApp?

Yes, when you use Meta's official WhatsApp Business Platform and follow its policies, which is how we build every bot. Unofficial auto-reply tools can get your number banned.

Can I send messages to all my customers?

You can send reminders, notifications and offers using Meta-approved message templates, to customers who agreed to receive your messages. Meta charges fees for some of these message types. You pay them directly to Meta, separately from our subscription, and we explain them before we start.

Does the bot connect to my current systems?

Yes. We connect it to your booking system, CRM or Google Sheets via APIs and webhooks, so data moves automatically with no manual entry.

How long does it take to set up the bot?

It depends on the number of departments and flows. Before we start you get a clear timeline, and the free trial begins once the flows are approved with you.
