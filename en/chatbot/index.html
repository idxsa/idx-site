Frequently asked questions

Do I need a new number?

No. Your current business number can be connected to Meta's official WhatsApp Business Platform. Before we start, we explain what the connection requires and how it affects how you use the number today. We can also set up a new number if you prefer.

Can the bot reply in Saudi dialect?

Yes. We write the replies in your business's voice, in Saudi dialect, Modern Standard Arabic or English, and refine them with you.

What if a customer asks something the bot doesn't know?

It hands the chat to an employee with a summary, so the customer doesn't have to repeat themselves.

What's the difference between the bot and the AI agent?

The bot follows set paths we design with you, which suits booking and qualifying. The AI agent understands open questions and answers from your business information. You can combine both.

How much does it cost?

It depends on the number of departments and flows. We start with a 14-day free trial, then a fixed monthly subscription agreed before we begin.
