Frequently asked questions

How much does a website cost?

It depends on the number of pages, languages and integrations you need. After a free consultation you get a clear quote with the scope of work and timeline.

How long does it take to build a website?

A landing page is much faster than a full website. Before we start you get a timeline with dates for every phase.

What's the difference between a landing page and a website?

A landing page is a single page for a specific ad or offer, built to get visitors to contact you. A website presents your whole business, with a page for each service that helps you show up on Google.

Will my website show up on Google right away?

We set it up technically and register it with Google from day one, so its pages start appearing within days to weeks. Reaching the top results takes time and ongoing content, and nobody can guarantee it.

Does the website work on mobile?

Yes. We design for mobile first, since most visitors come from their phones, then adapt it for desktop.

Can you improve my current website?

Yes. We start with a free review of your current site, show you what it's missing, then suggest improvements or a rebuild as needed.
