Your data is shared only with the organization you are dealing with through our service (for example, your owners-association manager and supervisor), and with service providers that host or deliver the service for us: Meta Platforms (WhatsApp Business Platform) and our cloud database and hosting providers. Storage and security

Data is stored in secured cloud databases with access limited to authorized staff. We keep data only as long as needed to provide the service or as required by law.

Your rights and data deletion

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your data at any time. See Data Deletion Instructions, or email info@idx.sa. We respond within 30 days. This policy follows the Saudi Personal Data Protection Law (PDPL).
